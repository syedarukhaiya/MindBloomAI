# Hackathon Submission

## Problem
Indian youth often carry academic, career, family and social pressure while stigma makes support difficult.

## Solution
MindBloomAI creates a private first step between silent struggle and supported action.

## Innovation
Bloom Context Engine + safety-first orchestration + transparent pattern detection + micro-interventions + healthy garden + multilingual support + human-support bridge.

## Google Cloud
Real Vertex AI Gemini integration via `google-genai` is the production AI provider.

## Impact
Measure check-ins, reflections, intervention completion, support-resource clicks and reported usefulness; do not claim clinical outcomes without validation.
